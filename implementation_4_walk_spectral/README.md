# implementation_4_walk_spectral — quantum-walk spectral classifier (QW-SC)

A quantum neural network whose trainable part is an observable diagonal in the
eigenbasis of a continuous-time quantum walk, benchmarked on `bars_and_stripes`
from the qml-benchmarks suite (Bowles, Ahmed & Schuld 2024) under that suite's
protocol and data.

## Model

For an $L\times L$ image $x$ ($L = 2^m$):

1. **Encoding.** $|\psi_x\rangle = x/\lVert x\rVert$ on $2m$ qubits (row register ⊗ column register).
2. **Walk.** The pixel graph is the torus $C_L \,\square\, C_L$ with CTQW Hamiltonian
   $H = A_r\otimes I + I\otimes A_c$, $A$ the cycle adjacency, eigenvalues
   $\lambda_k = 2\cos(2\pi k/L)$, eigenvectors the Fourier modes. $\Pi_a(A)$ is the
   spectral projector onto $\lambda_a$, $a = 0,\dots,L/2$.
3. **Trainable observable.** $O_w = \sum_{a,b} w_{ab}\,\Pi_a(A_r)\otimes\Pi_b(A_c)$, an
   arbitrary real function of the two commuting walk Hamiltonians;
   $(L/2+1)^2$ parameters.
4. **Output.** $f_w(x) = \langle\psi_x|O_w|\psi_x\rangle + b$, label $\operatorname{sgn} f_w$.

**Circuit.** StatePreparation → QFT on each register (the unitary that diagonalises
the cycle CTQW) → computational-basis measurement → average $w_{\mathrm{bin}(k_r),\mathrm{bin}(k_c)}$.

## Statements (all checked numerically in `verify.py`)

- **P1 (the walk's role).** $[O_w, e^{-iHt}] = 0$ for all $t$: $f_w$ is a conserved
  quantity of the walk, the input state's distribution over walk energies.
  The QFT diagonalises $A$ exactly (check 1); commutator $2\times10^{-16}$ (check 2).
- **P2 (function class).** $f_w(x) = x^{\mathsf T}Mx/\lVert x\rVert^2 + b$ with $M$ ranging over the
  algebra generated by $A_r, A_c$: every quadratic form invariant under the
  translations and reflections of the torus.
- **P3 (the target is in the class).** A noise-free stripe (rows constant) lies in
  $\operatorname{range}\,\Pi_0(A_c)$ and a bar in $\operatorname{range}\,\Pi_0(A_r)$. With
  $w_{a0} = +1$ ($a\neq0$), $w_{0b} = -1$ ($b\neq0$), all else 0:
  $f = \pm(1-\mu^2)$, $\mu^2$ the fraction of the image's norm in the constant mode. This is zero
  only for uniform images, which the generator produces identically for both
  labels. Check 4: 0 errors on 7,735 non-uniform noise-free images, $L = 4\ldots32$.
- **P4 (trainability).** $f_w$ is linear in $(w,b)$, so the logistic loss is convex: a
  unique global optimum and no barren plateau, by construction.
- **P5 (shots).** $\lvert f_w\rvert \le \max\lvert w\rvert$ (learned: 0.8, 4.7, 1.4, 0.1 for $L = 4, 8, 16, 32$), so
  $O(\max\lvert w\rvert^2/\varepsilon^2)$ shots per input, independent of image size. With 100 shots
  the accuracy is unchanged at every size.
- **P6 (limits).** The features are the image's 2D power spectrum, classically
  computable in $O(N\log N)$. No quantum advantage is claimed. Cost is dominated by
  amplitude encoding: $\Theta(N)$ CX (11 / 57 / 247 / 1013 CX for $L = 4 / 8 / 16 / 32$, transpiled,
  level 3), against 6 / 12 / 24 / 40 CX for the walk layer, on 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 qubits.

## Results (test accuracy, 5 seeds, GridSearchCV 5-fold on the training set)

| size | CNN (classical) | QuanvolutionalNN | WeiNet | **QW-SC** | QW-SC, 100 shots | Bayes ceiling* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4×4 | 0.955 | 0.925 | 0.545 | **0.930** | 0.937 | 0.930–0.970 |
| 8×8 | 1.000 | 0.996 | 0.503 | **1.000** | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| 16×16 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.561 | **1.000** | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| 32×32 | 1.000 | not run | 0.503 | **1.000** | 1.000 | 1.000 |

Baselines are the suite's published results (`paper/results/bars_and_stripes`).
*4×4: 20 of the 200 test images are uniform, so their labels are coin flips. The lower
bound (0.930) is Bayes-optimal on the other 180 images plus the training-set majority
rule on the uniform ones. The upper bound (0.970) assumes the rule for uniform images
is chosen with hindsight on the test set. QW-SC gets 178/180 on the learnable images,
the same as the Bayes-optimal classifier.

## Files

`data.py` (exact regeneration of the suite's CSVs, seed 42, plus the Bayes classifier),
`qwnn.py` (model, scikit-learn / qml-benchmarks interface), `verify.py` (checks 1–5),
`run_benchmark.py` → `results.json`. Requires numpy, scipy, scikit-learn, qiskit ≥ 2.
The data generator is copied from qml-benchmarks (Apache-2.0).
