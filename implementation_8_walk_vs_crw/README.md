# implementation_8_walk_vs_crw — quantum walks against classical random walks

`walk_vs_crw.py` → `fig_walk_crw.pdf/.png`, `tab_walk_crw.tex`, `walk_vs_crw.json`.
Used by Section 3.2 of the thesis (*Comparison with Classical Random Walks*).

Exact arithmetic only (probability vectors, state vectors, one density matrix); nothing is sampled.
Needs NumPy, SciPy (`jv`, `iv`) and matplotlib. Runs in about a minute.

What is computed
- Line, discrete time: the symmetric ±1 random walk (binomial law) and the Hadamard walk from
  the symmetric coin state (|0⟩ + i|1⟩)/√2, iterated to t = 200. Konno's weak-limit density is overlaid.
- Line, continuous time: the classical walk with rate 1 per edge, p_x(t) = e^{-2t} I_x(2t), and the
  quantum walk generated by the adjacency matrix, ψ_x(t) = (−i)^x J_x(2t). The arcsine limit density is overlaid.
- Spread: the standard deviation of the position against t for all four, and for the Hadamard walk whose coin
  is dephased with probability q per step (q = 0.02, 0.1, 1; q = 1 reproduces the classical walk exactly).
- Cycle C_64, continuous time: the classical distribution at t = 400 (stationary to 7e-4), the quantum
  distribution at t = 400, and the quantum time-averaged distribution, which is checked against the closed form
  (2N−2)/N² at the start vertex and its antipode and (N−2)/N² elsewhere (agreement 1e-16).

Numbers quoted in the text are in `walk_vs_crw.json`.
